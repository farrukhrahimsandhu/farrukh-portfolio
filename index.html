1. Search Intelligence Data Contract

The Problem: Querying massive Parquet files locally takes too much time and storage.

2. Predicting Traffic Drops (Baseline Action Scores)

The Problem: Flagging underperforming content before applying complex ML models.

The Action: Designed a baseline rule (Search Volume > 500, CTR < 2%) and removed future-window metrics to prevent Data Leakage.

The Result: Exported a prioritized queue of actionable targets. This is the honest benchmark my future models must beat.
